# Novelty Questions

## Current novelty status

`NOT_AUDITED — NO NOVELTY CLAIM AUTHORIZED`

The central threat is that an existing scholarly knowledge graph, mathematical knowledge base, theorem-management system, or provenance-aware claim system may already combine the proposed capabilities. The paper must therefore distinguish the vision through evidence, not labels.

## Primary question

Why is this not merely another scholarly knowledge graph?

## Questions the audit must answer

1. Do prior systems model theorem-level claims as primary objects, or mainly papers, entities, abstracts, concepts, and citation edges?
2. Is theorem **scope** explicit and queryable across formal axes such as data/combined complexity, fixed/input roles, query class, structural restriction, preprocessing/query time, randomness, semantics, domain, and parameter assumptions?
3. Are assumptions and restrictions attached to individual claims rather than inferred from paper-level context?
4. Is every promoted claim bound to an exact source location or equivalent verifiable evidence pointer?
5. Are multiple paper and theorem versions represented as lineage rather than duplicate documents?
6. Are theorem relations typed and individually justified by evidence?
7. Does the system distinguish unknown, not applicable, not stated, and verified values without silently filling gaps?
8. Does it use a fail-closed promotion model with human-author verification?
9. Is the representation tailored to database-theory distinctions that materially change theorem meaning?
10. Does the system support audit and reproducibility at the record level?

## Candidate differentiators to test, not assert

- scope preservation;
- evidence grounding;
- version awareness;
- theorem-level database-theory modeling;
- formal scope axes;
- typed, evidence-justified theoretical relations;
- fail-closed scientific record promotion;
- human/AI governance of uncertain records.

## Novelty Kill Gate

After the formal audit, issue exactly one verdict:

- **GO:** no close prior system implements the material combination; the contribution can be defended with precise comparisons.
- **CONDITIONAL_GO:** important components exist; narrow the thesis and contribution claims to the defensible residual difference.
- **NO_GO:** a close prior system already implements scope-preserving, theorem-level, version-aware, source-grounded records with justified typed relations, leaving no meaningful paper-level distinction.

No full-paper drafting may begin until this verdict and its evidence matrix are recorded.
